01 — About

About Gary Yu-Ho Yeh

Gary Yu-Ho Yeh (葉欲禾) is a PhD candidate in Information Management at National Sun Yat-sen University and a visiting scholar in Business Information Technology at Virginia Tech. His research sits at the intersection of information systems, human–AI interaction and sustainability, combining experimental methods with configurational analysis (fsQCA) to understand how people and organizations adopt and adapt to emerging technologies. His work appears in outlets such as the International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction and the Journal of Electronic Commerce Research, and in the proceedings of AMCIS, PACIS, ECIS and HICSS.

02 — Research

Research Interests

A few threads run through my work. Each pairs a behavioural or organizational question with a methodological commitment to experiments and configurational analysis.

Human–AI Interaction

How people read, trust and work alongside AI agents — from teammate framing and service recovery to the tone an AI should take when it is wrong.

Human–AI InteractionTrust RepairAI Teammates

Generative AI Services

Designing and evaluating GenAI-enabled services — conversational agents, AI customer service quality, and AI presenters in health communication.

GenAI ServicesAI in Healthcare & NursingService Quality

Digital Transformation

What makes transformation stick inside organizations: leadership logics, employees' digital dexterity, and the twin digital-and-green transformation agenda.

Digital TransformationDigital DexterityLeadership

Sustainability

Why sustainable intentions so often fail to become sustainable action, and what resources, norms and identities close that gap for consumers and firms.

Sustainable ConsumptionSustainable BusinessGreen Transformation

Urban Environments & Ageing

How built environments, public transit and active mobility shape active ageing and residential satisfaction in vehicle-dominated Asian cities.

Urban PlanningActive AgeingActive Mobility

Methods

Experimental and quasi-experimental designs paired with fuzzy-set qualitative comparative analysis (fsQCA) for configurational, rather than net-effect, explanations.

fsQCAExperimental ResearchSEM

07 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Gary Yu-Ho Yeh research?

His research covers human–AI interaction, generative AI services, digital transformation, sustainable consumption and business, and how urban built environments shape active ageing. A recurring question across these areas is why people's intentions — to trust an AI, to adopt a technology, to act sustainably — so often fail to translate into behaviour.

Which research methods does he use?

Primarily experimental and quasi-experimental designs combined with fuzzy-set qualitative comparative analysis (fsQCA), and structural equation modelling (SEM). fsQCA lets him look for configurations of conditions that jointly produce an outcome, rather than the average effect of one variable at a time.

Where are his teaching materials available?

The interactive textbook on experimental research methods and statistics is free to read online, along with slide decks on generative AI and on big-data analysis of healthcare information systems. They are all linked in the Teaching section of this page.
