07How this page is kept honest

  1. Every item is read from the journal's own editorial or call page, not from a database export. Where a publisher blocked access, the summary rests on the abstract or metadata and says so in the Notion notes.
  2. Editor-written agenda pieces that publishers label as research notes or discussion papers are included and flagged, because they do the work of an editorial.
  3. The eight directions are assigned from the direction tags recorded for each item; an item can sit in several. The counts on this page are generated from that file, never typed by hand.
  4. Refreshed quarterly from the IS Editorial Watch notes on Notion, which is where corrections should land first.
