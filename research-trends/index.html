03Rising and cooling

Share of each year's editorials. 2026 runs to 9 only.

Editors are asking for more of this

  • Agentic AI and delegated autonomy — an organizational problem, not an interface one (JMIS, I&M, JAIS, JSIS)
  • Written GenAI policy — prohibition versus managed adoption (ISJ vs MS, JIT, ISR)
  • Policy as a research object — enforcement, post-deployment risk (JAIS, JIT, MISQ)
  • Futures and speculative inquiry — now a site of research, not commentary (JIT, ISJ, EJIS)
  • Cognitive consequences — offloading effort erodes judgment (ISR, JAIS, MS)

Editors are arguing against this

  • The metaverse — defended as not dead (JMIS, DSS)
  • Blockchain and decentralisation — now a governance design choice (JIT)
  • Individual-level AI acceptance — declared insufficient on its own (JMIS, JSIS)
  • Vaguely labelled “AI” — the artefact has to be described (ISJ, EJIS)
  • Transformation as a destination — reframed as continuous (JSIS, ISJ)
  • Narrow Green IT — absorbed into responsible technology (MISQ, JAIS)
